About the vehicle replays

Compare two vehicles or change one setup and watch it run again. The simulations cover acceleration, braking, steady corners, braking into a corner and accelerating out of it. Results are model estimates. A quarter-mile result uses the ESS one-foot rollout convention, while the animation clock starts at first motion.
