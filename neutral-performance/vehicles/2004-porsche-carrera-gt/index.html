2004 Porsche Carrera GT

2004 example in the 2003–06 production series. Factory series specifications; market-specific test conditions are unspecified.

The 2004 Porsche Carrera GT is a mid-engine, rear-drive car rated at 604 hp (PS (DIN)) and 435 lb-ft, with a 6-speed manual with ceramic clutch. The manufacturer lists 3,042 lb curb weight — about 5.0 lb per horsepower.
