Z06 · 2-door fixed-roof coupe · 6-speed manual · US

The 2006 Chevrolet Corvette Z06 is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 505 hp and 470 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 3,132 lb curb weight — about 6.2 lb per horsepower.
