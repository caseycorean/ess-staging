2003 Dodge Viper SRT-10 Roadster

US SRT-10 roadster with six-speed manual. The weight is for the road-test example.

The 2003 Dodge Viper SRT-10 Roadster is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 500 hp and 525 lb-ft, with a 6-speed manual. The cited road test weighed 3,408 lb as tested weight — about 6.8 lb per horsepower.
