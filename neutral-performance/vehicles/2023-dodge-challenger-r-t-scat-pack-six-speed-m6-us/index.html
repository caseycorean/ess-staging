2023 Dodge Challenger R/T Scat Pack

R/T Scat Pack, standard body · 2-door coupe · 6-speed manual · US

The 2023 Dodge Challenger R/T Scat Pack is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 485 hp and 475 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 4,223 lb curb weight with 55% on the front axle — about 8.7 lb per horsepower.
