Raptor, standard 33-inch tires · 4-door SuperCrew pickup · 10-speed automatic · US

The 2024 Ford Ranger Raptor is a all-wheel-drive car rated at 405 hp and 430 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 5,325 lb curb weight — about 13.1 lb per horsepower.
