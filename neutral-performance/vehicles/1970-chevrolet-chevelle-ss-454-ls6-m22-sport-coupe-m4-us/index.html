1970 Chevrolet Chevelle SS 454 LS6

SS 454 LS6, heavy-duty close-ratio manual, standard axle · 2-door Sport Coupe (13637) · 4-speed manual · US

The 1970 Chevrolet Chevelle SS 454 LS6 is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 450 hp (gross) and 500 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 3,706 lb curb weight with 56% on the front axle — about 8.2 lb per horsepower.
