1969 Ford Mustang Mach 1

Mach 1, 428 Cobra Jet and 3.91 axle · 2-door SportsRoof · 3-speed automatic · US

The 1969 Ford Mustang Mach 1 is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 335 hp (gross) and 440 lb-ft. The cited road test weighed 3,607 lb as tested weight — about 10.8 lb per horsepower.
