McLaren F1 LM

F1 LM road-going edition. Values are from the LM column of the specialist reference and are kept distinct from the GTR and standard F1.

The McLaren F1 LM is a mid-engine, rear-drive car rated at 680 hp (bhp), with a transverse six-speed with limited-slip differential. Published figures give 2,341 lb weight — about 3.4 lb per horsepower.
