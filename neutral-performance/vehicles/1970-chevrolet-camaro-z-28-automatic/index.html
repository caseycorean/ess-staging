1970 Chevrolet Camaro Z/28

Early second-generation Z/28 with a three-speed automatic. Engine ratings are period gross figures. Curb figure is the road-test car.

The 1970 Chevrolet Camaro Z/28 is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 360 hp (gross) and 370 lb-ft, with a 3-speed automatic. The cited road test weighed 3,640 lb as tested weight — about 10.1 lb per horsepower.
