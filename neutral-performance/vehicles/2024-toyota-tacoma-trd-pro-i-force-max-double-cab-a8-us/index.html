TRD Pro, i-FORCE MAX hybrid, 5-foot bed · 4-door Double Cab pickup · 8-speed automatic · US

The 2024 Toyota Tacoma TRD Pro is a all-wheel-drive car rated at 326 hp and 465 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 5,100 lb curb weight — about 15.6 lb per horsepower.
