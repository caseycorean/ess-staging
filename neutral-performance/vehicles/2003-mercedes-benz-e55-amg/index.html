2003 Mercedes-Benz E55 AMG

US W211 sedan tested with the supercharged V8.

The 2003 Mercedes-Benz E55 AMG is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 469 hp and 516 lb-ft, with a 5-speed automatic. The cited road test weighed 4,243 lb as tested weight — about 9.0 lb per horsepower.
