ACR with Extreme Aero · 2-door coupe · 6-speed manual · US

The 2017 Dodge Viper ACR Extreme Aero is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 645 hp and 600 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 3,392 lb curb weight — about 5.3 lb per horsepower.
