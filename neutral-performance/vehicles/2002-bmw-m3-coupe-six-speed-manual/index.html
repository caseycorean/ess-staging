2002 BMW M3 Coupe

US 2002 E46 M3 coupe. BMW’s model-year sheet identifies the coupe separately from the convertible. Published figures use BMW’s curb-weight convention.

The 2002 BMW M3 Coupe is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 333 hp and 262 lb-ft, with a 6-speed manual. The manufacturer lists 3,415 lb curb weight with 50% on the front axle — about 10.3 lb per horsepower.
