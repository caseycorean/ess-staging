2023 Toyota GR Corolla Circuit Edition

Circuit Edition · 5-door hatchback · 6-speed manual · US

The 2023 Toyota GR Corolla Circuit Edition is a all-wheel-drive car rated at 300 hp and 273 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 3,285 lb curb weight — about 10.9 lb per horsepower.
