GT Premium 401A, Performance Package, active exhaust, MagneRide, Recaro and Nite Pony (C/D test configuration) · 2-door fastback · 6-speed manual · US

The 2024 Ford Mustang GT Premium Performance Package is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 486 hp and 418 lb-ft. The cited road test weighed 3,947 lb as tested weight — about 8.1 lb per horsepower.
