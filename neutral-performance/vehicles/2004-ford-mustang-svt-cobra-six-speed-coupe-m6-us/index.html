2004 Ford Mustang SVT Cobra

SVT Cobra · 2-door coupe · 6-speed manual · US

The 2004 Ford Mustang SVT Cobra is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 390 hp and 390 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 3,665 lb curb weight — about 9.4 lb per horsepower.
