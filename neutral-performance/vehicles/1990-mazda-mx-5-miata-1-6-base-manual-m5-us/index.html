1990 Mazda MX-5 Miata

Base, no equipment package · 2-door soft-top roadster · 5-speed manual · US

The 1990 Mazda MX-5 Miata is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 116 hp and 100 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 2,160 lb curb weight — about 18.6 lb per horsepower.
