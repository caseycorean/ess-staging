2017 Nissan GT-R Premium

Premium, standard summer tires · 2-door coupe · 6-speed automatic · US

The 2017 Nissan GT-R Premium is a all-wheel-drive car rated at 565 hp and 467 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 3,928 lb curb weight with 54% on the front axle — about 7.0 lb per horsepower.
