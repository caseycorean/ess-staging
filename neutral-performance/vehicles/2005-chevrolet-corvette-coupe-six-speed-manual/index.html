2005 Chevrolet Corvette Coupe

US 2005 C6 coupe with the six-speed manual. Base coupe dimensions and weight shown. The archive does not identify the Z51 option, so package-specific gearing and test figures are omitted.

The 2005 Chevrolet Corvette Coupe is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 400 hp and 400 lb-ft, with a 6-speed manual. The manufacturer lists 3,179 lb curb weight — about 7.9 lb per horsepower.
