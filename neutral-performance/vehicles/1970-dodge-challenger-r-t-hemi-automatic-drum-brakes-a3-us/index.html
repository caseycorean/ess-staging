1970 Dodge Challenger R/T Hemi

R/T Hemi, 3.23 axle and drums; test car optional cold-air hood · 2-door hardtop · 3-speed automatic · US

The 1970 Dodge Challenger R/T Hemi is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 425 hp (gross) and 490 lb-ft. The cited road test weighed 3,890 lb as tested weight — about 9.2 lb per horsepower.
