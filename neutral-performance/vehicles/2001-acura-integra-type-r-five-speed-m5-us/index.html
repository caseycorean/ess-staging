2001 Acura Integra Type R

Type R · 3-door hatchback · 5-speed manual · US

The 2001 Acura Integra Type R is a front-wheel-drive car rated at 195 hp and 130 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 2,639 lb curb weight with 62% on the front axle — about 13.5 lb per horsepower.
