What This Calculator Does

This car weight distribution calculator helps estimate how moving components like batteries, fuel cells, engines, and lightweight body panels affects front-to-rear balance.

It is designed as a practical ballpark tool for builders who want to understand whether a change is likely to make a small, meaningful, or major difference before getting the car on scales.
