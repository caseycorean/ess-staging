About ESS

Explore the car you have.

Supercar X started with the idea that any car could be worth studying. Endicott Speed Shop carries that idea forward with sourced vehicle specifications, explanations and experiments.

Use ESS to research a configuration, ask what a change might do, and decide what to measure or test next. The result you want might be more grip, a better ride, a more useful gear or simply a clearer understanding of how your vehicle works.

What beta means

We expand coverage when the evidence supports it. If a source conflicts, a model needs an input or a specification is missing, we show that distinction. Tell us what needs attention.
