2015 Chevrolet Camaro Z/28

Z/28 · 2-door coupe · 6-speed manual · US

The 2015 Chevrolet Camaro Z/28 is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 505 hp and 481 lb-ft. The cited road test weighed 3,861 lb as tested weight — about 7.6 lb per horsepower.
