2004 Ferrari Challenge Stradale

Road-going Challenge Stradale. Its F1 gearbox and test state are distinct from the 360 Challenge competition car.

The 2004 Ferrari Challenge Stradale is a mid-engine, rear-drive car rated at 425 hp, with a f1 paddle-shift automated manual. The cited road test weighed 3,152 lb as tested weight — about 7.4 lb per horsepower.
