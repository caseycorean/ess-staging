Engineering research

Build a case for the change.

Endicott Speed Shop grew out of Supercar X and a simple question. How much can we learn about the potential of the car we already have?

Start with the car and its job

A daily-driver hatchback, a tow-capable pickup and a track car have different goals. Establish a baseline for the one you have. Record the exact configuration, the equipment, and the conditions that matter.

Model one change

Use a shared model to see what changing gearing, tires, weight or suspension might do. Distinguish factory specifications, independent tests and your own measurements from estimates.

Repeat the measurement

Check the prediction under comparable conditions. Capture the setup and what changed, including results that challenge the model.
