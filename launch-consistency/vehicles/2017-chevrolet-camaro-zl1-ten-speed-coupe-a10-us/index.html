2017 Chevrolet Camaro ZL1

ZL1, automatic; standard ZL1 chassis · 2-door coupe · 10-speed automatic · US

The 2017 Chevrolet Camaro ZL1 is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 650 hp and 650 lb-ft. The cited road test weighed 3,933 lb as tested weight with 55% on the front axle — about 6.1 lb per horsepower.
