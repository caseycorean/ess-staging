2022 Porsche 718 Cayman GT4 RS

GT4 RS, standard composite iron discs · 2-door coupe · 7-speed automatic · US

The 2022 Porsche 718 Cayman GT4 RS is a mid-engine, rear-drive car rated at 493 hp and 331 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 3,227 lb curb weight — about 6.5 lb per horsepower.
