2004 Ford F-150 SVT Lightning

SVT Lightning · 2-door regular cab pickup · 4-speed automatic · US

The 2004 Ford F-150 SVT Lightning is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 380 hp and 450 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 4,670 lb curb weight — about 12.3 lb per horsepower.
