1991 Ferrari F40

1991 F40 road-test car. Weight is the publication’s test-car value.

The 1991 Ferrari F40 is a mid-engine, rear-drive car rated at 478 hp and 424 lb-ft, with a 5-speed manual. The cited road test weighed 3,018 lb as tested weight — about 6.3 lb per horsepower.
