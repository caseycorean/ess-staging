2023 Honda Civic Type R

Type R, standard wheels and tires · 5-door hatchback · 6-speed manual · US

The 2023 Honda Civic Type R is a front-wheel-drive car rated at 315 hp and 310 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 3,188 lb curb weight with 62% on the front axle — about 10.1 lb per horsepower.
