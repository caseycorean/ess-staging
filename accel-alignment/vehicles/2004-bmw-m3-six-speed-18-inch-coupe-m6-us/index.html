2004 BMW M3

M3, standard 18-inch wheels · 2-door coupe · 6-speed manual · US

The 2004 BMW M3 is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 333 hp and 262 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 3,415 lb curb weight with 50% on the front axle — about 10.3 lb per horsepower.
