2021 Ram 1500 TRX

TRX, factory nominal 35-inch tires · 4-door Crew Cab pickup · 8-speed automatic · US

The 2021 Ram 1500 TRX is a all-wheel-drive car rated at 702 hp and 650 lb-ft. The manufacturer lists 6,350 lb curb weight — about 9.0 lb per horsepower.
