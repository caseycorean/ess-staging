2003 Ferrari 575M Maranello F1

575M road car with the optional F1 gearbox. This road test and the competition 575 GTC are separate sources.

The 2003 Ferrari 575M Maranello F1 is a front-engine, rear-drive car rated at 508 hp and 434 lb-ft, with a f1 paddle-shift automated manual. The cited road test weighed 4,085 lb as tested weight — about 8.0 lb per horsepower.
